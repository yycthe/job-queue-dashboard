Private local file. Copy to CANDIDATE_PROFILE.md, never commit the completed copy.

## Evidence labels

USER_CONFIRMED: explicitly stated or confirmed by the candidate.
DOCUMENT_SUPPORTED: supported by a named source document.
INFERRED: qualified inference, never presented as confirmed.
UNKNOWN: not established; do not invent a positive answer.

For each useful fact, retain the value, evidence label, source, confirmation date, scope, and qualifications.
